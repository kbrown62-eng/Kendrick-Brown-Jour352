The Value Of Religion

An analysis of America's current religious trends

By Kendrick Brown

April 4, 2026

Church attendance is going down in America, with 44% of people almost never attending in the year 2022 according to reports by The American Bible Organization. There's also a decline in terms of religious education in the home and in the schools as 56% of people aren't praying with their children in a study done by the Religious Landscape Study of US adults.
